## Governance
The original 2025 Charter remains the baseline, not automatically ratified. The Community Board of Review is a **proposed** learning function subject to the charter's existing Membership Assembly, Board of Trustees, and Council of Deans and Deacons. Owner approval gates production and institutional claims.
