# ADR-0001 — Student-authored Journey first

**Status:** Proposed for Founder decision.

**Context:** MMSP 2025 treats the Temple curriculum as a spiral and invites individualized study. The present vision prioritizes learner sovereignty, cooperative mentors, experiential learning, community review, and service.

**Decision proposed:** Treat the Journey Record, not fixed degree/major enrollment, as the central content and interaction entity. The first book is the test case. The existing Charter's governing bodies remain foundational; a Community Board of Review has no independent charter authority until approved.

**Consequences:** Flexible paths, per-student evidence, mentor matching, review snapshots; significant privacy/quality governance required before production. No issuance of authentic school credentials in prototype.
